Linux Distributions and AI Specialization
-----------------------------------------
Many distributions now offer specialized versions or tools for AI, often referred to as "AI-ready" Linux: 

Ubuntu AI Edition: Preloaded with TensorFlow, PyTorch, and other essential tools.

Fedora AI Spin: Tailored for AI education and research.

Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL) AI: A dedicated platform for running large language models (LLMs) in enterprise environments, integrating with OpenShift AI.

SUSE Linux Enterprise Server 16: Designed with AI-assisted infrastructure management (MCP-aligned).
